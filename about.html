About me

My Philosopy

I began my career as a newspaper reporter in Washington, D.C., but discovered quickly that the skills I'd developed in that role translated extremely well into a profession I was much more passionate about: Technical documentation.

As a technical writer, I believe strongly that our job is not simply to explain complex topics clearly and concisely, but that we are also called to be our customers' strongest advocates within our organizations. We bridge the gap between developers and users, translating oftentimes vague or complicated tickets and design specs into readable, useful, and credible documentation that users feel safe using as their primary information resource.

When we are good at what we do, we enable our users to work independently, decrease the volume of Support calls, and create content that can also be used as a marketing tool. Simply put, good docs lead to happier customers and increased sales. It's a win-win.

When I'm not writing world-class enterprise documentation, I enjoy reading, collecting comic books, cooking, traveling, and being a total pop culture nerd.
